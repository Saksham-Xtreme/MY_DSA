### Revision Cheat Sheet
- **Rules of Big-O**:
  - Drop constants: $O(2N) \rightarrow O(N)$.
  - Drop non-dominant terms: $O(N^2 + N) \rightarrow O(N^2)$.
- **Standard Complexities**: $O(1) < O(\log N) < O(N) < O(N \log N) < O(N^2) < O(2^N) < O(N!)$.
